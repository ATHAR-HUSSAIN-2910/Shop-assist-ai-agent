# Shipping Policy

## Order Processing Time

Orders are processed within **1–2 business days** after payment
confirmation.

Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed on the
next business day.

## Shipping Partners

Orders are shipped through one of our delivery partners:

- BlueDart
- Delhivery
- DTDC
- India Post (for remote pin codes)

The delivery partner is assigned automatically based on the delivery
location and is shown on the order page once the order is shipped.

## Delivery Timelines

Estimated delivery times after dispatch:

- **Metro cities:** 2–4 business days
- **Tier 2 / Tier 3 cities:** 4–7 business days
- **Remote / Rural areas:** 7–12 business days

These are estimates and may change due to carrier delays, weather,
operational issues, or other unexpected circumstances.

## Tracking an Order

Once an order is shipped, a tracking number is generated and
displayed on the customer's order page.

Customers can use this tracking number on the carrier's website to
monitor delivery progress.

If tracking information has not updated for more than **48 hours**,
the customer can contact support and the case will be escalated to
the carrier.

## Delivery Attempts

The delivery partner will attempt delivery up to **3 times**.

If all attempts fail, the order is returned to the warehouse and the
customer is notified. Re-shipment may be requested from the order
page (additional shipping charges may apply).

## Incorrect Address

Customers are responsible for providing a correct delivery address.

If an order is returned due to an incorrect or incomplete address,
the customer must update the address and request re-shipment.
Shipping charges for the second attempt are not refundable.

## Delayed or Lost Shipments

If a shipment is delayed beyond the estimated delivery window by more
than **5 business days**, the customer can request an investigation.

If the shipment is confirmed lost by the carrier, the customer can
choose either:

- A full refund, or
- A free replacement shipment

## Shipping Charges

Shipping charges (if any) are shown at checkout and depend on the
delivery location and order value.

Orders above a certain value may qualify for free shipping. This
threshold is shown on the product and checkout pages.

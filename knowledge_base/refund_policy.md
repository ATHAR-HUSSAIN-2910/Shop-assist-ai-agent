# Refund Policy

## When Refunds Are Issued

A refund is issued only after a returned product has been received
and successfully passed inspection.

If the inspection fails (for example, the product is damaged or
missing accessories), the refund will not be issued and the product
will be returned to the customer.

## Refund Processing Time

Once the refund is approved, it is initiated within **1 business day**.

After initiation, the amount is credited back to the customer's
original payment method within:

- **Credit / Debit Card:** 5–7 business days
- **UPI / Net Banking:** 3–5 business days
- **Cash on Delivery (COD):** 7–10 business days (refunded via bank transfer)

## Refund Status

Customers can track the refund status from their order page under
the "Refunds" section.

If a refund has not been received within the timeframes listed above,
the customer can contact support and the case will be escalated for
investigation.

## Partial Refunds

In some cases, only a partial refund may be issued. This can happen if:

- The product is returned without original packaging or accessories
- The product shows signs of use beyond normal inspection
- Only part of a multi-item order is returned

The exact refund amount will be communicated to the customer before
the refund is processed.

## Cancellation Refunds

If an order is cancelled before it is shipped, the full amount is
refunded automatically. Refunds for cancelled orders follow the same
timelines listed above.
